Executive Insight

Clifton represents one of the strongest young-adult ministry opportunities in the Great Miami River District. The combination of education, mobility, culture, and professional development creates significant opportunities for relational discipleship and community formation.

Understanding the Community

The Clifton neighborhood sits within one of Cincinnati's most dynamic urban environments. Universities, medical centers, and cultural organizations contribute to a community characterized by learning, innovation, and movement.

Many residents are making major decisions regarding education, careers, relationships, and future direction. The church has a unique opportunity to help people establish meaningful relationships and spiritual roots during seasons of transition.
